## Removed prior wording

The untested “ninety seconds” setup promise, “same Wi-Fi” joining promise, and
“quick” outcome promise were removed from the public copy. The product still
provides a room code and explicit calibration path, but does not promise a time
or network topology it cannot verify in every home.
